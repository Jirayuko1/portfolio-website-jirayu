# Product Context: Jirayu Portfolio

## Purpose & Goal
A flagship personal portfolio website for **Jirayu**, showcasing senior capabilities as a **Full-Stack & Mobile Developer**. The site shifts away from generic developer templates and presents an interactive, memorable 3D Office Experience where visitors explore case studies, credentials, and engineering philosophy through physical office artifacts.

## Target Audience
- Engineering Directors & Tech Leads looking for reliable, high-craft engineers.
- Founders & Product Managers seeking engineers who clarify requirements before writing code.
- Design-conscious clients looking for polished Web & Mobile products.

## Core Identity & Positioning
- **Role:** Full-Stack & Mobile Developer (iOS, Android, React Native, Next.js, Node.js, Cloud).
- **Soft Skills / Philosophy:** *"Ask First, Code Later"* — proactively questioning, analyzing edge cases, and clarifying specifications before implementation to eliminate rework. High ownership, continuous learner.
- **Hard Skills:** Seamlessly bridges mobile client applications and complex cloud web architectures.

## Mode
- **Experience & Persuade:** An immersive 3D spatial canvas that lets visitors interact with objects (Bookshelf, Telephone, Zuko the Cat, Corkboard, World Globe) to discover work at their own pace.
